## Completeness finding

This repository is **not a complete archive of the certificate classwork**. It contains one small retrospective app showing a subset of the technologies. It does not contain the learner's original work for all 15 courses, including the Node/Express, Flask/AI, Kubernetes/OpenShift, capstone, and assessment material.

To archive the actual classwork, the original learner-authored notebooks, source files, project submissions, reports, or other saved work are needed. Keep the repository's existing distinction between the independent LaunchOps project and Coursera submissions. Add only files you are permitted to publish; do not add course-provided starter code, private assessments, or answer keys unless their terms allow redistribution.
